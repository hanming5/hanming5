## Engineering Principles

> **Simplicity is a feature.** The best system is the one the next engineer understands on first read.

1. **Start from the problem, not the tool.** Technology choices follow user needs and business context.
2. **Design for change.** Clear boundaries and well-defined contracts keep systems adaptable.
3. **Make it observable.** If it cannot be measured, it cannot be improved — or trusted.
4. **Ship in small, safe steps.** Incremental delivery reduces risk and accelerates feedback.
5. **Leave it better than you found it.** Code, documentation, and teams alike.
